After Rain / 晴光轻奏
Original procedural composition created for Stillwater, 2026-10-20.

96-second instrumental loop, 60 BPM. Soft felt-keyboard-like notes, widely spaced
melody, gentle major/add9 harmony and a low ambient pad with diffuse reverb.
No third-party recording, sampled instrument, copyrighted melody, noise bed or
external music service is used. No external music attribution is required.

Source: scripts/compose_music.py (numpy + ffmpeg, creation time only).
Delivery: after-rain.mp3, 32 kHz stereo, 96 kbit/s, ~1.1 MB. Loaded only after a
user starts playback. HTML media streaming avoids a full decoded music buffer
and continuous runtime synthesizer work. Music starts muted and pauses when
this page is hidden; its clock does not depend on rendering frame rate.
